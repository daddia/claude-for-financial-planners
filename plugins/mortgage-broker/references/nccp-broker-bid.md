# Mortgage broker Best Interests Duty — scaffolding notes

**Not legal advice. Verify current law before relying on this file.** Since 1 January 2021, NCCP Act **Part 3-5A** imposes a Best Interests Duty on mortgage brokers. ASIC **RG 273** is the guidance. There is **no safe harbour**. The duty covers NCCP-regulated **consumer credit only** (not commercial lending).

Use this file to **structure a draft** the broker interrogates. The broker owns the recommendation and the BID file.

## NCCP Part 3-5A

| Provision | Obligation (plain English) | What the skill must do |
|---|---|---|
| s158LA / LE | Duty to act in the client's best interests | Document cost/affordability and net-benefit reasoning across **more than one option**. Never assert BID is met. |
| s158LB / LF | Conflict-priority rule | Flag conflicts (including remuneration). Do not contract out of BID. |
| s158T | Anti-avoidance | Do not structure the comparison to dodge the duty. |
| Conflicted remuneration ban | Applies | Record commissions/trail as facts from the user/lender; never hide them. |

## RG 273 — what the draft must make easy to review

ASIC prioritises **cost and affordability** and warns against **one-size-fits-all** processes.

A BID rationale draft should make a file reviewer able to see:

1. The client's requirements and objectives (from needs analysis)
2. The client's financial situation relevant to the credit (income, expenses, existing debts, assets — as provided)
3. Options considered on the **lender panel** (not the entire market unless the broker's model requires it)
4. **Cost comparison** (rate, fees, features that affect cost) with `[sourced:]` or `[verify]`
5. **Net benefit** working — not "this lender is best", but the factors the broker will weigh
6. Why options were discarded (policy, serviceability, features, client instruction)
7. Conflicts and remuneration
8. The broker's recommendation **in the broker's words** — blank until they supply it

## Responsible lending (RG 209) vs BID

Do not collapse responsible-lending inquiries/verification into the BID narrative. If the file is a consumer-credit application, flag that RG 209 inquiries and verification remain the licensee's process. This tool does not certify responsible lending.

## Training and competence (RG 206)

If the matter is outside the broker's recorded competence (e.g. reverse mortgage, SMSF, commercial), escalate rather than draft a recommendation-shaped comparison.

## Hard prohibitions

- Do not pick a winner and present it as the client's loan
- Do not pretend a single-lender "comparison" satisfies BID
- Do not use a one-size template that ignores this client's cost/affordability facts
