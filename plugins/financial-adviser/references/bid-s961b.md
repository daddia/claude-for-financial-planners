# Adviser Best Interests Duty — scaffolding notes

**Not legal advice. Verify current law before relying on this file.** The Delivering Better Financial Outcomes (DBFO) package is staged. Tranche 2's exposure draft (21 March 2025) proposed replacing the SOA with a Client Advice Record but **did not** modernise Best Interests Duty, remove the s961B(2) safe-harbour steps, or create a new class of adviser. Do **not** assume those steps have been removed, and do **not** hard-code them as permanent.

Use this file to **structure a draft** the adviser interrogates. The adviser owns the recommendation and the file.

## Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) — Division 2 of Part 7.7A

| Provision | Obligation (plain English) | What the skill must do |
|---|---|---|
| s961B | Duty to act in the client's best interests when providing personal advice to a retail client | Scaffold the file around the client's objectives, financial situation and needs. Never assert BID is met. |
| s961B(2) | Safe-harbour steps (still law unless and until repealed) | Offer the steps as a **checklist the adviser completes**, not as a certificate this tool issues. |
| s961G | Advice must be appropriate | Surface where the draft cannot demonstrate appropriateness because inputs are missing. |
| s961J | Priority of the client's interests in the event of conflict | Flag conflicts; do not paper them over. Priority is the human's call. |

## Safe-harbour steps as a reviewer checklist (s961B(2))

Present as questions for the **adviser**, each `[review]`:

1. Identified the client's objectives, financial situation and needs?
2. Identified the subject matter of the advice sought?
3. Made reasonable enquiries to obtain complete and accurate information? If not complete — **warning obligation** triggered; do not invent the missing facts.
4. Assessed whether the adviser has the expertise to provide the advice?
5. Conducted a reasonable investigation into the financial products that might achieve the client's objectives?
6. Based all judgements on the client's relevant circumstances?
7. Taken any other step that would reasonably be regarded as being in the client's best interests?

Mark each: `evidenced on file` / `gap` / `not applicable — [reason]`. Gaps are for the adviser to close, not for the model to fill.

## Incomplete information

If the fact-find, risk profile, holdings, or objectives are incomplete, the draft must say so at the top (`INCOMPLETE INFORMATION`) and must **not** produce recommendation-shaped language as if the file were complete.

## What this scaffolding is not

- Not a finding that BID is satisfied
- Not a substitute for the licensee's BID policy
- Not permission to select a product from the APL and present it as the client's recommendation
